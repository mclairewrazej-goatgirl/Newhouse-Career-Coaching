# Coaching Tracker: quick start

Your notes are saved in **your own Google Sheet**. The app shows and edits
them, and only someone with your password can sign in. Setup takes about
10 minutes and is done **once, on a computer**.

---

## Part 1: Set up (on a computer)

**1. Copy the sheet.** Sign in to your own Google account, open the link
you were sent, and click **Make a copy**. Wait about 10 seconds for a
**Coaching Tracker** menu to appear at the top (refresh if it doesn't).

[screenshot: Make a copy button]

**2. Prepare it.** Click **Coaching Tracker › 1. Prepare my sheet**. Google
asks for permission:

- Click **Continue** and pick your account.
- On "Google hasn't verified this app", click **Advanced › Go to Coaching
  Tracker (unsafe) › Allow**. It's your own copy, so this is expected.

Then click **1. Prepare my sheet** again. Six tabs appear.

[screenshot: "Google hasn't verified this app" with Advanced highlighted]

**3. Switch it on.**

1. Go to **Extensions › Apps Script**. Don't change anything you see.
2. Click the blue **Deploy** button, then **New deployment**.
3. Click the gear icon next to *Select type* and choose **Web app**.
4. Set **Execute as: Me** and **Who has access: Anyone**. Only someone with
   your password can see anything.
5. Click **Deploy**, then **Copy** the address that ends in `/exec`, then
   **Done**.

[screenshot: Deploy button]
[screenshot: New deployment settings, Execute as Me / Anyone]
[screenshot: the address ending in /exec with the Copy button]

**4. Choose your password.** Back in the sheet, click **Coaching Tracker ›
2. Finish setup**.

1. Paste the address from step 3 (it may already be filled in).
2. Enter your email and a password of 10 or more characters.
3. Click **Save and get my app link**.

[screenshot: Finish setup panel]

**5. Open the app.** Click **Open the app**, or copy your personal app link
and send it to yourself. Open that link once on your phone and once on each
computer you use, and sign in.

- **iPhone:** Share › **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** ⋮ › **Add to Home screen**.

[screenshot: personal app link with Copy button]

## Part 2: Every day

- **Schedule.** Use **+ Plan a session** to book someone. Add the Meet link
  if you have one, and you'll be told if that day already has a booking. On
  the day, tap **Start session**. For a session that wasn't planned, tap
  **Start unbooked session**.
- **During a session.** Tap **Join Google Meet**, check *Goals for today*
  (did they do it? did it help? If not done yet, **Carry over to next
  session** keeps it on the list), then tap **Begin**. Add points
  under *What you covered* and *Next steps*. Connect a mentor if one would
  help. When you're done, tap **End, save & log**, then book the next one.
  [screenshot: live session screen]
- **Clients.** Each client's page shows their total coaching time, every
  past session and their mentor connections. **Coaching summary (PDF)**
  makes a tidy document to save or send.
  [screenshot: coaching summary preview]
- **Mentors.** Keep the people you introduce clients to. On a client's
  page or during a session, mark **Did they make contact?** once you hear
  back. Choosing **Yes** asks when: tap **At this session** (or **Today**), or
  pick the date they reached out and tap **Save date**. If someone's been
  connected 3 or more times in 90 days, you'll see a "give them a break"
  warning.
- **Review.** Look back by week or month, and mark which suggestions
  helped.
- **No internet?** Keep working. Changes are sent to your sheet when you're
  back online. The **Account** tab shows if anything is waiting.

## Saving a coaching summary as a PDF

Open the client, tap **Coaching summary (PDF)**, then **Save as PDF or
print**:

- **iPhone:** tap the share button at the top of the print screen, then
  **Save to Files** or **Mail**.
- **Android:** choose **Save as PDF**, then the download button.
- **Computer:** choose **Save as PDF** as the destination, then **Save**.

---

## Forgot your password?

Open your sheet on a computer and choose **Coaching Tracker › Change email
or password**. You don't need the old password. Every device is signed out,
so sign in again with the new one.

## Something not working?

| What you see | Try this |
|---|---|
| "This app isn't linked to a sheet yet" | Open your personal app link on that device (sheet menu › **Show my app link**). |
| "Wrong email or password" | Check capitals and typos. After 5 wrong tries, wait 15 minutes or reset the password from the sheet. |
| "Can't reach your sheet" | Check your internet, then tap **Sync now** on the Account tab. |
| No **Coaching Tracker** menu | Refresh the sheet and wait 10 seconds. Make sure you're in your copy. |

Keep your sheet private: anyone you share it with can read your notes.
